## What the evidence says

_What real usage showed: numbers if your tool has analytics, counted behaviour if it does not. Put the signal that matters on screen._

- **Primary signal:** 9 visits generated 35 page views, with 3.89 views per visit, 3m 47s average duration, and a 22% bounce rate.
- **What moved:** Users explored beyond the landing page, averaging 3.89 page views per visit. Qualitative feedback was positive about the product concept and design, and users understood the core fight-camp value proposition.
- **What didn't:** New-user activation had friction. One tester dropped off at device connection because it was unclear that linking wearable data was optional. Users also wanted more ways to explore the product before signup, including competition-preparation content and educational actions from the landing page.
